Privacy policy.

How AION Tech Career handles information submitted through enquiries and job applications.

Information we collect

We collect the details you provide in forms, including your name, email, phone number, location, qualification, experience, role interest, message and uploaded resume.

How we use it

Your information is used to review enquiries, assess application fit, contact you about opportunities and maintain submission records.

Storage

Applications and enquiries are stored in the site's database. Resume uploads are stored under the application records used by the recruitment team.

Your choices

You can request correction or removal of submitted information by contacting careers@aioncareer.in.
